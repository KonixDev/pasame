Download Pasame

For Windows, Mac and Linux

On Mac, this version is not verified by Apple yet. See how to open it.
Nothing to install on the phone. Pasame goes on a computer; the phone just scans the code shown on the screen.
  1. Open Pasame on your computer and choose the files.
  2. The other person points their phone camera at the code on the screen.
  3. Done: the files download. And they can send files back to you.
