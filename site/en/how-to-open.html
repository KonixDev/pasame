How to open Pasame

The first time, your computer may warn you because Pasame is new and free. That's normal, and you only do this once.

On Windows

  1. Double-click Pasame-Windows.exe (it's in Downloads).
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
  4. If a “Windows Defender Firewall” window appears, click Allow access. It lets the phone see your computer.

On Mac

  1. Open Pasame-macOS.dmg and drag Pasame to Applications.
  2. Open Pasame from Applications. If it says it can't be opened, click OK (or Done).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway. That button only shows up for a while: if you don't see it, try step 2 again.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

Once Pasame is verified by Apple, you won't see any warning on Mac.

On the phone

Nothing to install. Point the camera at the code shown on the computer and tap the notice that appears.
